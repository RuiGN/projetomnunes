## Governance

- The clinic remains responsible for confirming controller roles, legal bases and retention before production activation.
- Proposed bases in this register are engineering constraints, not a legal opinion. Each clinic/jurisdiction must record the approved basis, controller allocation, retention trigger and approver before enabling the category.
- Regulated capabilities fail closed when professional or clinic evidence is absent, expired, revoked or not verified; old evidence may remain for accountability but cannot authorize current access.
- A subject-rights request cannot automatically erase a psychological record, restricted assessment material or regulatory evidence when an approved professional/legal retention duty or legal hold applies. The decision and retained scope require documented justification.
- A supplier is added only after security/privacy due diligence, data-processing terms, subprocessor review, location review and an exit plan.
- No category may be reused for advertising, model training, disciplinary scoring or another secondary purpose without a new documented assessment and valid basis.
