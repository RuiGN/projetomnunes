## Full-gate blocker outside the layout scope

`tests/test_integration_calendars.py::test_oauth_state_tamper_and_expiry_protection` failed while validating an untouched, freshly generated token, before its tamper checks.

`integrations/calendars.py:68` splits `payload + b'.' + raw_digest` using `rsplit(b'.', 1)`. A raw digest can itself contain the separator, causing rejection of legitimate generated tokens. An isolated database-free probe with test settings and synthetic UUIDs generated 100 fresh tokens and reproduced 12 rejections. No tokens or secrets are included in these durable evidence files. `git diff -- integrations/calendars.py` is empty.

This existing security-service defect was not changed during a visual-only migration. It requires a separately authorized correction and a fresh full gate before any affected acceptance checkbox can be closed. Do not rerun until lucky and conceal the intermittent failure.
